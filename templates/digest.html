What each day was about

Each run keeps the stories Jev scored most severe. One dot is one story, placed by theme. Click a theme to hide or show it in the daily log below.

Dot size is fixed. A darker dot means a higher Jev severity (0 minor to 3 severe).

The threads

Written by Claude from the run summaries and their sourced claims, and not checked by verify. Dates are when the story ran, not always when the event happened.

Market moves the runs priced

Each run prices the tags of its verified claims over the last closed session. A US tag is the sector ETF against SPY. A global tag is the global sector ETF against ACWI. Any other country is that country's whole market against ACWI. Weekend runs reuse Friday's session.

How this was made
  • One run of main.py per day, built into this page by digest.py from data/events.db. Each run reads GDELT's market stories for 6 hours, so a day here is a 6-hour snapshot, not the full day.
  • Claims are kept only when the search returned their URL and every number appears in the article. Claims marked "unverified" are ones Jev could not confirm for the week.
  • Story summaries are written by the research model and are not checked by verify. The headline, themes and threads on this page are written by Claude from them.
  • Jev scores severity from the headline and GDELT metadata, not the article. A tag needs Jev at least 0.75 sure of both the sector and the country. Market moves are observed, not proof the news caused them.
